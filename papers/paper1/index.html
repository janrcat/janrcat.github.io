Temporal Disturbance and Applied Paradoxology: A Case Study of Frank the Rabbit

October 2025 · Frank the Rabbit · Journal of Tangent Universe Studies
Download
  • Paper
Abstract

This paper examines the theoretical underpinnings of Frank the Rabbit’s interventions in the Tangent Universe, focusing on his pedagogical role in instructing Donnie Darko through 28 days of existential turbulence. The analysis situates Frank within a lineage of paradoxical mentors whose work blurs the boundaries between dream, death, and destiny. Using the Philosophy of Time Travel as a framework, this study interrogates the ethics of spectral supervision and the ontological instability of manipulated timelines. Ultimately, the paper concludes that while the rabbit’s didactic methods remain unorthodox, his contribution to the continuity of spacetime cannot be dismissed—nor can the enduring appeal of his grotesque professionalism.
