Ale the Rabbit

Adjunct Professor of Temporal Disturbance and Applied Paradoxology. My work examines the intersection of sleep disorders, teenage prophecy, and jet-engine meteorology. I lecture primarily at midnight, often through dreams. Current projects include Ethics of Manipulated Death and Time Loops for Fun and Profit. When not haunting suburban basements, I enjoy existential dread, carrot cake, and reminding the living that the world ends in precisely 28 days, six hours, forty-two minutes, and twelve seconds.
